# jump start haven

## How to Play
Use WASD or arrow keys to navigate the player. Collect coins and reach the end of the platforms to a "GOAL" sign to complete the game. Avoid enemies that could restart the game!

## Assets Used
- [player](https://ozzbit-games.itch.io/fantasy-character) by Ozzbit Games
- [mushroom](https://monopixelart.itch.io/forest-monsters-pixel-art) by MonoPixelArt
- [coins](https://totuslotus.itch.io/pixel-coins) from TotusLotus
- [background & tileset](https://theflavare.itch.io/forest-nature-fantasy-tileset) by The Flavare
- Sound effects and music from Pixabay
